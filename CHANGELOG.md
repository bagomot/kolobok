# Changelog

Each release's section is what Modrinth, CurseForge and GitHub show for it.

## 1.2.0

The first public release.

- Kolobok, the bread bun from the Russian fairy tale, baked in a Russian stove built from a furnace and 12 bricks with the rolling pin.
- He follows you, sits, keeps guard or wanders, rides on your shoulder or your head, and sings a song of his own in the syllables of real Russian words.
- Tame a freshly baked one by listening to his song to the end, then give him a bucket of milk.
- Every mob he gets away from adds a verse to his song and teaches him a perk; the songbook writes them down.
- Foxes hunt him; berries save him.
- Outfits: helmets, bast shoes, sunglasses, a crown, a wreath and a kokoshnik. In a metal helmet he sometimes dreams he is the giant Head from Pushkin's «Ruslan and Ludmila».
- Skills: a bindle to carry him in, a goat horn to call him, bowling mobs over with him, cheeks full of loot, bread crumbs, finding the way to a village, the chicken jockey.
- A warm stove heals him; going stale, soggy or tanned; death is final.
- 27 advancements in a tab of their own, «The Tale of Kolobok».
- The guide book «How to Bake a Kolobok», given to every player on first joining; a Modonomicon book when Modonomicon is installed.
- Optional support for Jade, JEI, REI and Modonomicon.
- English and Russian.
