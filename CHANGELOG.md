# Changelog

Each release's section is what Modrinth, CurseForge and GitHub show for it.

## 1.3.0

- On your shoulder or your head he goes with you through portals, `/tp` and ender pearls. On his own he never steps into a portal, and a fox or a chicken no longer carries him through one.
- You carry one Kolobok at a time; another one will not climb on, he takes offence.
- He will not jump down where he would break: off a cliff, onto unloaded ground or while you glide, and he says so. Gliding no longer throws him off. In water, lava or any other fluid he jumps off at once.
- If you log out, sleep or turn spectator, he gets down and sits; if you die, he follows. Either way the drop does not hurt him.
- While his owner is away from the server he dozes and keeps to himself, though he still runs from danger.
- A feather tickles him: he squeaks and giggles.
- He sways to the beat of his song, shivers when scared and wobbles when dizzy, and his hat leans with him.
- A fox hears his song out to the last note before it grabs him.
- Mobs his song annoys or he lures go after whoever carries him, never a creative or spectator player, and give up after 10 seconds. Mobs that cannot attack butt him instead.
- At most the 8 nearest animals come to his concerts, villagers and wandering traders too. Data packs can add listeners with the `kolobok:concert_animals` tag and relatives with `kolobok:kolobok_kin`.
- The way to a village: once a game day whatever the answer, and until then he repeats it. The search is much lighter on the server, and the crumbs show the way for 30 seconds.
- The stove: an item it does not take only shows what is in it rather than taking out what is in the oven, and the off hand gets its turn. A furnace named on an anvil keeps its name through the stove. Breaking the stove pays out the experience of what it cooked. The rolling pin no longer builds a stove where any of its blocks is protected.
- He no longer picks up shulker boxes, bundles, bindles or other things with contents, and will not fit into a bindle with his cheeks too full.
- A songbook of an unnamed Kolobok is titled in the language of whoever writes it.
- Only your own Kolobki write in chat; anyone else's talk in bubbles. The Head's dream talk follows the chattiness setting.
- REI tells the doughs apart, and Modonomicon's stove is laid out right.
- The jars name the oldest NeoForge, Fabric API, Jade, JEI, REI and Modonomicon they work with, and link to the source code and the issue tracker.
- Fixed: a Kolobok or a cat floating after a reload, a cat stopping short of the stove, a Kolobok stuck on a chicken, advancements lost while the baker was offline, an offline owner's pets taken for strangers, a crash on a broken `voiceVolume`, a Kolobok lost when another mod stopped his release from a bindle, a burst of notes after a freeze, and spectators getting the guide book, using the stove and the rolling pin or the beetroot bonus.
- Lighter on the server and the client: the stove no longer disturbs its neighbours every tick, and several memory leaks are gone.

## 1.2.0

The first public release.

- Kolobok, the bread bun from the Russian fairy tale, baked in a Russian stove built from a furnace and 12 bricks with the rolling pin.
- He follows you, sits, keeps guard or wanders, rides on your shoulder or your head, and sings a song of his own in the syllables of real Russian words.
- Tame a freshly baked one by listening to his song to the end, then give him a bucket of milk.
- Every mob he gets away from adds a verse to his song and teaches him a perk; the songbook writes them down.
- Foxes hunt him; berries save him.
- Outfits: helmets, bast shoes, sunglasses, a crown, a wreath and a kokoshnik. In a metal helmet he sometimes dreams he is the giant Head from Pushkin's «Ruslan and Ludmila».
- Skills: a bindle to carry him in, a goat horn to call him, bowling mobs over with him, cheeks full of loot, bread crumbs, finding the way to a village, the chicken jockey.
- A warm stove heals him; going stale, soggy or tanned; death is final.
- 27 advancements in a tab of their own, «The Tale of Kolobok».
- The guide book «How to Bake a Kolobok», given to every player on first joining; a Modonomicon book when Modonomicon is installed.
- Optional support for Jade, JEI, REI and Modonomicon.
- English and Russian.
